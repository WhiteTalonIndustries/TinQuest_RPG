📋 Printing Instructions for Avery 5371:

DOUBLE-SIDED PRINTING:
The cards are generated with fronts and backs alternating. Your printer needs to flip on the LONG EDGE.

Option 1 - Automatic Duplex:
1. Load Avery 5371 card stock
2. Select "Print on Both Sides" or "Duplex"
3. Choose "Flip on LONG EDGE" (not short edge)
4. Print at Actual Size (100%)

Option 2 - Manual (if option 1 doesn't work):
1. Print ONLY ODD pages (1, 3, 5...) - these are the FRONTS
2. Take printed sheet, flip it on the LONG EDGE (top becomes bottom)
3. Reinsert with blank side up
4. Print ONLY EVEN pages (2, 4, 6...) - these are the BACKS
5. Test with plain paper first!

Sheet Layout: Portrait, 2 across × 5 down (10 cards)
Card size: 3.5" wide × 2" tall each
